Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About the PCD Pharma Franchise

Find answers to common questions before you apply.

What is a PCD pharma franchise?

A PCD (Propaganda Cum Distribution) pharma franchise is a business arrangement in which a partner promotes and distributes a pharmaceutical company's products in an agreed territory, subject to contractual and regulatory requirements.

Does Dr Best offer distribution opportunities?

Yes. Dr Best's website invites prospective distribution partners to enquire about its product portfolio, marketing support and territory arrangements. Specific PCD terms should be confirmed directly with the team.

How much investment is required?

Dr Best says investment depends on the scale of operations. Contact the company for current minimum order values, stock requirements and a detailed commercial proposal.

Is monopoly or area exclusivity available?

The company's distributor page discusses exclusive distribution rights under an agreement. Availability, territory boundaries and conditions should be confirmed in writing before committing.

What documents and licences will I need?

Requirements depend on your business model and location. Pharmaceutical wholesale distribution typically requires applicable drug licences and other statutory registrations. Confirm your eligibility with Dr Best and your local licensing authority.

What product categories does Dr Best cover?

Its website lists antibiotics, antifungals, anti-diabetic, gastrointestinal, cardiac, nutraceutical, anti-inflammatory, neuropsychiatry and OTC products, among others.

Will I receive training and marketing support?

Dr Best's distributor FAQ states that it provides training and marketing support. Ask the team what is included in the specific partnership offer.

How do I apply?

Complete the enquiry form above or call +91 75270 38383. The company can explain current eligibility, product availability and next steps.
